Waypoints
⚠ Garmin .fpl route points are typically capped near 99 — reduce for panel GPS import.
Edit route
Hold and move any vertex — letter corners, entries, and exits (amber). Drag a small gray handle mid-segment to add a new point there and bend the line. Turn arcs rebuild around your edits. Changing text, size, anchor, or waypoint count regenerates the shape and clears edits.
Export
Import the GPX into ForeFlight via AirDrop / Files, or the .fpl onto a Garmin datacard / Garmin Pilot.
